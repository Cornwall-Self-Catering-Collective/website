No single business creates a complete Cornish holiday.

A great visit may include self-catering accommodation, a local restaurant and a family attraction. It could involve a surf lesson, a gallery and produce from a nearby farm shop. Each business shapes part of the experience.

Collaboration connects those parts.

When accommodation providers share local knowledge, guests can discover more. Joint offers can encourage them to try new activities. Seasonal recommendations help spread demand across the year and across different parts of Cornwall.

This increases the guest’s return on investment. They receive more than a place to sleep. They gain trusted ideas, smoother planning and experiences that feel personal. Their time is better spent. Their holiday feels richer.

The benefits also flow back into the sector. Referrals create new customers for local businesses. Shared insight helps operators understand changing demand. Collective training can raise standards. A united voice gives the industry greater strength when discussing policy and Cornwall’s future.

Collaboration also reduces isolation. Businesses can share solutions, learn from one another and respond more effectively to common challenges.

Cornwall’s visitor economy is deeply connected. Accommodation, hospitality, retail, attractions and local services all rely on each other.

By working together, we can offer guests greater value while keeping more of their spending within Cornwall. That creates stronger businesses, more memorable visits and a healthier sector for the long term.
