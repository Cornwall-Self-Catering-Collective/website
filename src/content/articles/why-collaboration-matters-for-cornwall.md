From dining in hearty eateries and visiting family attractions to unwinding in a comfortable self-catering accommodation after a day spent exploring Cornwall's stunning coastline, every Cornish holiday is shaped by the local businesses that help create a memorable visitor experience. This is why it is important for Cornish businesses to work together rather than independently, delivering an unforgettable stay that demonstrates the value of a holiday in Cornwall and encourages tourists to feel their hard-earned money is well spent. By creating a positive experience, local businesses can help sustain Cornwall's thriving tourism industry and encourage visitors to return year after year.

## **Creating better experiences for visitors**

![](/assets/uploads/istock-1290044934-web.jpg)

Rather than maintaining a purely competitive mindset, businesses can enhance the visitor experience by recommending other local businesses, sharing local knowledge, and highlighting hidden gems, even when not specifically asked. By providing access to resources and attractions that visitors may not have otherwise discovered, tourists can explore different parts of Cornwall they never knew existed. As a result, their trips feel more personalised and memorable, helping to create a holiday they will cherish for years to come. Simply directing customers and guests towards locally recommended attractions, restaurants, or providing directions can make a significant difference to their overall experience.

## **Supporting local businesses**

![](/assets/uploads/istock-2150735792-web.jpg)

Recommending local businesses creates wider economic benefit to Cornwall. Not only does it help strengthen the tourism sector by providing visitors with authentic experiences recommended by locals, but it also helps keep tourism spending within the county. People often gravitate towards well-known destinations, such as St Ives, or familiar high street shops and fast food chains. However, by suggesting alternative places to visit similar to St Ives or mouth-watering eateries dotted around Cornwall, businesses can encourage visitors to explore beyond the county’s most popularised hotspots. For example, recommendations included in self-catering accommodation welcome packs can introduce guests to other idyllic locations they may not have otherwise discovered. As more visitors support local businesses across Cornwall, the economic benefits are spread more evenly throughout the county, helping local communities thrive whilst generating a stronger voice for Cornwall's future.

### **Why it matters for local communities**

![](/assets/uploads/istock-174783714-web.jpg)

With many areas of Cornwall facing higher levels of deprivation than the average neighbourhood in England, these simple efforts to collaborate, share recommendations, and create better experiences for visitors can have a positive ripple effect throughout local Cornish communities. Cornwall's thriving tourism sector supports a wide range of jobs, from working in restaurants, attractions, and retail to housekeeping and maintenance. People's willingness to experience the magnificence of Cornwall helps create a stable circular economy, supporting the growth of local businesses and improving the infrastructure that many communities desperately need.

By working together, we can demonstrate Cornwall's value as a popular tourism destination  time and time again, while encouraging visitor spending within the local economy. This not only enhances guests' holiday experiences but also improves the lives of Cornish communities for years to come.
